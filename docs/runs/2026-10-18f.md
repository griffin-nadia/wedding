## 1. Paper texture

- **First pass (#140).** Baked tiles of Paper's PaperTexture (no image), per theme, laid under the card. Jehan's notes: "texture not looking good", "more subtle, over all the content including the UI", "the crease could be more subtle", "create it, not an image", "invert from dark to light", "shouldn't have colour, more organic, higher quality, keep in lab for now".
- **Now (#142).** The paper is made in the browser with Paper's shader, as on shaders.paper.design:
  - One still sheet the size of the screen, at its pixel density, once per theme (`src/lib/paper-live.ts`). It's copied out as `--paper-live`, and the WebGL context is let go straight away.
  - No image is downloaded for it.
  - It lays over every paper surface, so the words, fields and buttons are printed on it.
  - By day: white paper with neutral grey grain, multiplied in. At night: inverted and screened in, so the walnut lifts.
  - Preset: fibre slightly larger and stronger than the grain, a few drops, folds 0.06, wrinkles 0.1.
- **The guest site is plain paper for now.** The made paper runs only in the lab. Once picked, it starts on the first tap. Made earlier, its large paint counted as the page's LCP: phone Home measured 3.1 s, against 1.8 s when started on input.
- **Lab:** Paper has four presets in both themes, with sliders and Copy settings, plus Nadia's crosshatch for comparison. Her crosshatch stays in her drawn envelope either way.
- All baked paper images and the bake script are gone. `@paper-design/shaders-react` is now a dependency.

## 2. The reply (#141)

- **Steps.** Who's coming → Food → Song (up to 2) → Your travel dates and Anything else → the check. With nobody coming it's Who → a note → the check. "Step x of y" and the segments follow.
- **One heading per step,** and that heading is the question. The heading's short rule is gone inside the sheet's head. It was the line peeking behind the progress bar.
- **Enter** moves to the next field, or puts the keyboard away after the last. It never moves the step on.
- **Keyboard up:** Back and Next stay just above the keyboard. The focused field scrolls to sit above them, once the new size is laid out and again after the keyboard rises. Switching keyboards only ever makes the sheet shorter, so nothing jumps.
- **Anything else** grows to 4 lines, then scrolls inside, keeping the caret in view.
- **Song search:**
  - Searches as you type (300 ms, from 2 letters), plus the button.
  - Picking a song closes the list, empties the field and adds a row: artwork, title, artist, remove.
  - The artwork is kept on the device, and looked up once for songs saved elsewhere.
  - Use what I typed stays last.
