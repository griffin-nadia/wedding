## 1. Arrival screen and music start

- **Music** (`src/components/music-toggle.tsx`): on by default as before. The first touch, click or key anywhere
  except the toggle now starts it at once (the 2 s wait is gone). It listens for pointerdown, touchend, click and
  keydown, because an iPhone only unlocks sound on a touch's touchend, and stops listening once it's playing.
  The fade goes from 0 to 0.3 over 2.5 s on an ease-out curve. It's clamped at 0.35 in the one place that sets the
  volume. Switching on from the toggle takes 0.5 s. Off is remembered and respected.
- The song preloads as soon as the page loads, whether the music is on or off.
- While it's on but the browser hasn't allowed sound yet, the toggle has a slow ring (`data-waiting`). Under reduced
  motion the ring is a still outline instead.
- "Remember the guest's level": nothing on the site lets a guest change the level (phone volume buttons change the
  phone's volume, which the page can't see and the phone keeps anyway), so there's nothing to store yet. If a
  level slider is wanted, it's a small addition: say so and it goes in the lab first.
- **Arrival** (`src/components/envelope.tsx`, `src/styles/polish.css`): the handwritten note and arrow are gone,
  replaced by one label under the envelope, "Open your invite", which is a real button with a gentle breathe.
  - The envelope floats: a 6px bob up and down and a 1° tilt over 4 s, with a shadow underneath that softens as it
    rises.
  - It lifts a little on hover or touch. Opening pauses the float where it is, so the open animation starts
    without a jump.
  - With reduced motion turned on, the float and the breathe don't move.
  - It sits lower-middle: on phones the envelope's top is at about 65% of the screen height, on laptops at about
    46%.
- **Long-press**: the images were already `draggable=false` and nothing wraps them in a link. Now there's also no
  touch-callout, no text selection, and the context menu is blocked on the arrival screen, so a long press never
  offers to save the picture.
- `en.ts` letter.note is now "Open your invite" (the brief's words); the Japanese stays 「招待状をひらいてね」.
  `arrival-note.tsx` and `lib/face.ts` were deleted along with their CSS.
- **Checks**:
  - `e2e/music.spec.ts` gains two tests:
    - The first tap starts it within 1.5 s, it's past 0.22 at 1.25 s and tops out between 0.29 and 0.35, and the
      waiting pulse goes once it's playing.
    - From off, the toggle reaches 0.3 within 1.2 s.
  - New `e2e/arrival.spec.ts` (motion on) checks:
    - the label is a button
    - no link around the envelope, images not draggable, no callout or select
    - it floats, sits below 30% of the screen and pauses on opening
    - the letter gets focus
  - All 92 e2e tests pass locally, plus 27 unit tests and lint (warnings only).
  - Looked at 320, 390 (iPhone 13 profile) and 1440, light and dark.
- Merged as #128.

## 2. Brand colour and texture

- **Names as masks** (`names-art.tsx`, `scripts/art-export.mjs`): Nadia's black lettering is exported as two alpha
  masks on the same frame, `names-mask-1040.webp` (53 kB) and `amp-mask-1040.webp` (3 kB). The "&" is split off at
  the clear columns either side of it (x 990 to 1150 of 2000).
  - The names are filled with `--ink`: brown (kogecha) by day, cream (kinari) in Lantern.
  - The "&" is filled with `--action`: sage ink by day, sage in Lantern.
  - The envelope is a `.paper` surface, so it always takes the day tokens, which is why the `tone` prop went.
  - The brown and cream picture exports (8 files) were deleted.
- **Walnut Lantern** (`tokens.css`, only the four night surfaces moved; `theme-color` and the first paint follow):

  | Token | Was | Now |
  |---|---|---|
  | `--brand-yoru` (paper) | #22160e | #2e1b12 `oklch(24.5% 0.034 49)` |
  | `--brand-yoru-raised` | #2f2016 | #382317 `oklch(27.8% 0.038 50)` |
  | `--brand-yoru-fill` | #3d2b1d | #462e1f `oklch(32.5% 0.043 53)` |
  | `--brand-yoru-line` | #5a4230 | #654936 `oklch(43% 0.048 55)` |

  - Contrast on the new paper: cream ink 13.4:1, muted 6.5:1, sage 6.3:1, rust 6.7:1.
  - On raised: cream ink 12.1:1, muted 5.9:1, sage 5.7:1.
  - On fill: cream ink 10.3:1, muted 5.0:1, sage 4.9:1.
  - The pumpkin story colour (4.85 on paper, 4.4 on raised) is only ever a line or map stroke, which needs 3:1.
  - Before and after: [travel 390 before](18e/walnut-before-travel-390-lantern.jpg),
    [after](18e/walnut-after-travel-390-lantern.jpg); [home 1440 before](18e/walnut-before-home-1440-lantern.jpg),
    [after](18e/walnut-after-home-1440-lantern.jpg).
- **Paper tile** (`scripts/paper-tile.mjs`): Nadia's crosshatch, sampled from a 660px plain patch of the closed
  envelope's left flap (x 320, y 1580 of 4000).
  - Turned into strokes only (how much darker each pixel is than the paper around it), shrunk to 512px, and made
    seamless by crossfading it with itself shifted half a tile.
  - Baked opaque, so it compresses: `texture-hatch-light.webp` (17 kB) is warm strokes on white at strength 0.16,
    drawn with multiply. `texture-hatch-lantern.webp` (16 kB) is pale strokes on black at 0.12, drawn with screen.
    Below 10% of the strongest stroke counts as paper.
  - With alpha it was 110 to 140 kB, because WebP and AVIF keep alpha lossless.
  - It replaces the washi fibres (the token is `--texture-hatch`, with `--paper-blend`) on every paper-feel card,
    the fortune slip and the sent letter.
  - The overlay on the drawn envelope is gone, since her envelope already has the hatching.
  - The fibre tiles (54 kB) and their bake were deleted.
- Compared in the lab's micro-grain page, now labelled "Nadia's paper (her crosshatch)": the site's value is the
  full baked strength (slider at 1), and the slider goes below it.
- The first try sampled the seal's edge by mistake, and the first token name, `--texture-paper`, clashed with the
  laptop letter's SVG grain. Both were caught (the RSVP paper test caught the name clash) and fixed.
- **Checks**: 92 e2e tests and 27 unit tests pass. The RSVP paper test now looks for the crosshatch tile.
- Merged as #129.

## 3. Favicon from the wax seal

- `scripts/favicon.mjs` (from `private/nadia-art-17oct/wax-seal.png`, nothing private copied):
  - The seal is trimmed and stepped down by halves (one jump to 16px loses its edge).
  - At 16 and 32 it's drawn edge to edge with a little extra contrast.
  - Outputs: `favicon.ico` (16, 32 and 48 as PNGs, 8 kB); `icon-192.png` and `icon-512.png` (transparent);
    `icon-512-maskable.png` (the seal at 62% on the cream, inside the safe circle); `apple-touch-icon.png` (180, on
    the cream, since a phone fills transparency with black).
  - `index.html` points to the .ico and the 192 PNG. The old `favicon.svg` (the old mark) is gone. The manifest lists
    192, 512 and the maskable 512.
- At 16px it reads as a red wax seal, a round red blob with the scalloped edge. The G N monogram shows from 32px up;
  at 16 there aren't enough pixels for letters, so the shape does the work.
  [At 1x and 4x, light and dark](18e/favicon-sizes.png).
- Check: `e2e/icons.spec.ts`. Every icon link and the manifest return 200; the .ico holds 16, 32 and 48; each
  manifest icon decodes at the size it claims; one is maskable.
