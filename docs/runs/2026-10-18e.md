## 1. Arrival screen and music start

- **Music** (`src/components/music-toggle.tsx`): on by default as before. The first touch, click or key anywhere
  except the toggle now starts it at once (the 2 s wait is gone). It listens for pointerdown, touchend, click and
  keydown, because an iPhone only unlocks sound on a touch's touchend, and stops listening once it's playing.
  The fade goes from 0 to 0.3 over 2.5 s on an ease-out curve. It's clamped at 0.35 in the one place that sets the

## 7. Our story on laptops: one input model

- `story-journey.tsx`: the arrow buttons, the arrow keys and now a trackpad swipe each step exactly one stop, all
  through the same `go()` and spring. Before this, a trackpad swipe did nothing.
  - A sideways swipe moves once its push passes 24px. Then it's locked: the momentum events that keep arriving,
    smaller and smaller, are ignored until the wheel has been quiet for 200 ms, so a tail never adds a second stop.
  - Up and down stays the page's (the card's scroll).
  - The browser's own swipe-back is held off on the story.
  - Keys and buttons step from where it's heading (`target`), not the last render, so quick presses never skip
    or repeat a stop.
- Test (`story.spec.ts`, laptop): a swipe with a 16-event momentum tail is one stop, forward, forward, back. A
  vertical wheel is none. Three ArrowRight presses are three stops, ArrowLeft is one back, two quick clicks are two.
- 102 e2e tests pass.
