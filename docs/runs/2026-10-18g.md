## 1. Paper (#150)

- **Jehan's settings are the "chosen" preset:** blending 0.4, distortion -0.15, angle 300, seed 4, roughness 0.4/0.5 (rows 0), fiber 0.4/0.5, folds 0, crumples 0 (count 6), wrinkles 1/0.65, drops 0.4, scale 0.9.
  - Lantern: drops 0.2 and wrinkles 0.6.
  - The 18f organic pick, very subtle, and no folds or wrinkles sit beside it in the lab.
- **Neutral colours:** white paper with grey grain, multiplied in by day; inverted and screened in Lantern.
- **On the site:**
  - It's made 3 s after load at idle, or on the first input, whichever comes first, and fades in over 400 ms.
  - It covers the cards, the sheets (so the fortune), the driver card and the night letter.
  - It stays plain under reduced motion or Save-Data, or without WebGL.
- **AA on its darkest spot:** the strength is capped so `--ink-muted` on the paper stays 4.5:1 on the sheet's darkest spot by day (lightest at night). That measured 0.14 by day, because the drops make dark specks, and 0.30 in Lantern.
- **The LCP gate passes** (1.82 s on phone Home). The 3 s wait matters: starting it at the first idle pushed phone Home to 3.5 s in the software-WebGL run, the same as that run's baseline without the paper.

## 2. Music (#151)

- **Web Audio:** the song goes through a MediaElementSource into a GainNode, so the level and the fades work on iOS Safari, which ignores `audio.volume`. The graph is made inside the first tap.
- **Level:** target 0.2, capped at 0.25. Fades in over 2.5 s on first start and over 0.5 s back on the tab. Starting on the first tap, stopping when hidden, and Media Session are unchanged.
- **Loudness** (ffmpeg loudnorm, two passes, linear, so one constant gain and the loop stays seamless):
  - Before: -17.03 LUFS, true peak +0.09 dB, LRA 11.7.
  - After: -20.44 LUFS, true peak -3.32 dB, 96 kbps, 2.9 MB.
- **`e2e/music.spec`** reads the gain node, and checks the element stays at 1.
