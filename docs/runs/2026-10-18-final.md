## Found on the way

- **The live back end and the new pages.** The live back end (version 43 until today) didn't know `?action=questions`
  or `?action=journeys`, and answers an unknown action with `{ ok: true }` and nothing else. The FAQs (#160) and Our
  story's last stop (#157) read the missing list as if it were there and would have crashed (a blank page) between
  the merge and the Apps Script push. Both now treat a missing list as none, and so does the flying count; the stress
  test covers it.
- **The city wasn't being saved.** Since #156 went live the site sends a city, and the old back end refused it
  cleanly (bad_request, nothing written). Pushed the back end from main straight after (version 44), rather than
  waiting for every back-end PR.
- **Swipes on Android.** `touch-action` stops at the nearest scroll container, so the letter's own scroller let
  Chrome cancel a sideways drag (#163).
- **The globe on 4-core devices.** "4 cores or fewer starts flat" sent CI's runners, and any browser that reports
  4, to the flat map; it's under 4 now (#157).
- **The envelope's letter in Lantern** was blended the day way (multiply) because it sat inside the envelope's day
  tokens (#165).
