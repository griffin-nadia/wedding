## 5. Consistency sweep

Measured what's on screen (.brief/tools/sweep.mjs): every page plus the reply and Hotels sheets, at 390 and
1440, every visible element's font size, padding, margin and gap, and corner radius. Then grepped the source
for literal sizes and off-scale Tailwind steps.

| Distinct rendered values | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Font sizes | 9 (6 roles; the clamped roles land on 2 values each) | 9 (the same 6 roles, no one-offs) |
| Spacing values | 22 | 18 |
| Off the space scale | 13 (2, 6, 10, 20, 40 and 8 layout sums) | 9 (a 2px tick-box nudge and 8 layout sums) |
| Corner radii | 8 | 5 (8, 12 for the nesting rule, 16, 24, pill) |

- Font sizes: literal sizes in the source went from 5 to 1 (the fortune's 大吉, part of its drawing). The

  map's own units, since they scale with the drawing.
- Spacing snapped to `--space-*`: the dock's icon gap (2 → 4), Travel's jump-link strip, the trail text, the
  arrival note, tick rows (10 → 8, still 44 tall), the Kyoto map tag (3/8 → 4/8), the RSVP button in the bar

- Left as they are, on purpose: sums the layout works out (the letter's left offset, the dock band, a sheet

## 6. Music check

- Hidden, locked, swiped away or frozen already stopped the song at once (visibilitychange, pagehide, freeze →
  pause with no fade), but the media session was left as "paused" with the title and artwork still set, which
  is what keeps the iPhone lock screen's Now Playing and Android's media notification hanging around.
- Now the hard stop also sets `navigator.mediaSession.metadata = null`, `playbackState = "none"` and removes
  the play / pause handlers. Coming back (visible or pageshow) plays again and sets them afresh. Pausing with
  the toggle while on the page keeps the entry as paused, so the lock screen can still resume it.
- New gate e2e/music.spec.ts: starts the song with a tap, fires pagehide, expects "none" and no title. It
  fails on the old code (paused, title kept) and passes now.
- Needs a real phone: headless Chromium has the Media Session API but no lock screen, so the entry clearing on
  an actual iPhone and Android phone is still worth a look.
