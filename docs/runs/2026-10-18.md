## 2. Mobile: no jumping

Measured on a 390 phone with probes (.brief/tools/jump*.mjs, sheet-return.mjs) before changing anything:
- Home's RSVP, Change my reply, the reply chips, FAQs rows and the Travel sheets didn't move the page.
- Real jump 1: opening a row while another row above it was open. The one above closes and pulls the tapped
  row up by up to 400px (Travel 422 → 18). Fixed once in the shared Disclosure: while the one above closes,
  the page scrolls with it, so the tapped row stays under your finger (now 422 → 422 on every pair).
  FAQs still collapses visibly when you're already at the top of that short page (nothing to scroll back).
- Real jump 2: Travel's jump links, the three summary lines and "See where to stay" were instant browser
  jumps. They now glide (smooth scroll), land at once under reduced motion, and replace the hash rather than
  adding history.
- Real jump 3: a sheet didn't put the page back. With the page behind moved 300px while open (what a phone
  keyboard does), main stayed at 890 after closing; now every sheet returns to 590 exactly. The shared sheet
  notes the scroll position and opener as it opens, then returns focus without scrolling and restores the
  position. The reply sheet's focus moves no longer scroll the page either (preventScroll).
- New gate: e2e/no-jump.spec.ts (phone) covers jumps 1 and 3.
- Default: headless iPhone emulation isn't iOS Safari, so a check on a real iPhone is still worth doing.
