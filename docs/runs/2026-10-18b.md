## 1. Top bar: always there, never merges

- Before: on phones, あ / A, music and the sun or moon sat inside the letter's top right corner (3 buttons in the
  card); the letter started 8px from the top of the screen. From 768 they were already in the top bar.
- Now one top bar on every screen: G&N on the left, the three switches on the right (plus the nav and RSVP from
  768, as before). Nothing of it is inside the card any more (0 buttons in the letter).
- It never hides. The only change on scroll: a hairline under it and a little more paper (78% → 94%), from a
  single `data-scrolled` attribute written only when it changes.
- Phones: 56px plus the top safe area (the notch), padding for the side safe areas in landscape. The sticky page
  header now sticks under the bar (it read the bar's height, not a fixed 64), and Travel's jump offset includes it.
- The bar's paper is its own layer (`::before`), so its blur doesn't trap the phone dock (a blurred parent becomes
  a fixed child's containing block).
- Check: `e2e/top-bar.spec.ts`. The switches are in the bar and not in the letter, and the bar stays at y = 0 and
  visible through scrolls down and back up, with the hairline on once scrolled. Fails on the old phone layout.
