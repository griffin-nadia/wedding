## 1. Top bar: always there, never merges

- Before: on phones, あ / A, music and the sun or moon sat inside the letter's top right corner (3 buttons in the
  card); the letter started 8px from the top of the screen. From 768 they were already in the top bar.
- Now one top bar on every screen: G&N on the left, the three switches on the right (plus the nav and RSVP from
  768, as before). Nothing of it is inside the card any more (0 buttons in the letter).
- It never hides. The only change on scroll: a hairline under it and a little more paper (78% → 94%), from a
  single `data-scrolled` attribute written only when it changes.
- Phones: 56px plus the top safe area (the notch), padding for the side safe areas in landscape. The sticky page
  header now sticks under the bar (it read the bar's height, not a fixed 64), and Travel's jump offset includes it.
- The bar's paper is its own layer (`::before`), so its blur doesn't trap the phone dock (a blurred parent becomes
  a fixed child's containing block).
- Check: `e2e/top-bar.spec.ts`. The switches are in the bar and not in the letter, and the bar stays at y = 0 and
  visible through scrolls down and back up, with the hairline on once scrolled. Fails on the old phone layout.
- Merged as #101.

## 2. No jumps or flashes anywhere

Measured first (`.brief/tools/s2-measure.mjs`, `circle-origin.mjs`, `swipe-white.mjs`): five page changes through
the nav on each device and theme, the card's edges and the nav's position each frame, layout shift, and frames with
near-white pixels.

| | Before | After |
|---|---|---|
| Card top, laptop: Home vs the other pages | 132 vs 96 | 96 everywhere |
| Short pages' card bottom, phone (Home, FAQs) | 675 and 689, 90 to 104px above the dock | 767, 12px above the dock, same as long pages |
| Short pages' card bottom, laptop | 737 to 858 | at least 836 everywhere (64 from the bottom) |
| Nav movement during a page change | 0 | 0 |
| Layout shift on Home, laptop | 0.0043 | 0.0006 |
| Frames with white, tab taps and swipes (headless) | 0 | 0 |

- Page height: every card is at least the screen between the top bar and the dock (phones, `svh` so it never grows
  as the browser bar hides) or the bottom margin (laptops). Home on laptops no longer sits lower than the others; it
  starts at the same 96 and runs to the same bottom (the sign-off sits at the foot).
- White flash: headless Chromium never showed one, so this is the iPhone's own paint. The `html` element now carries
  the theme's paper (set inline in `index.html` before any CSS loads, then from the tokens), the body has no background
  of its own (so the photo still shows), `color-scheme` follows the theme so native overscroll and scrollbars match,
  and theme-color already matched and updates on switch. A check compares the page background to theme-color in
  both themes.
- Bottom of the page on phones: the strip that repainted the photo over the letter above the dock is gone. It was
  positioned from heights JS wrote as the browser bar moved, which is the stutter. Now the content simply has a gap
  of dock + safe area + 12 above the dock, and a soft 24px paper fade along the dock's top edge for anything
  scrolling under it. The phone made-by mark lived in that strip, so it goes (section 8's FAQ says who made the
  site).
- Light / dark circle: Chromium drew it from the button on the first switch already, on both the old and new
  builds, so the first-use bug is the iPhone (Safari) not applying a script animation to the transition on its
  first run, leaving the stylesheet's `circle(0 at 50% 50%)`. The circle's resting clip and its keyframes are now
  written in literal pixels at the button before the transition starts; nothing is animated from script and nothing
  is centred in the middle any more. Measured: the first changed frame is centred within 5px of the button (phone
  and laptop, first and third switch). Needs the real-phone check.
- Checks: `e2e/page-change.spec.ts`. The card's top and visible bottom are identical across all five pages (phone
  and laptop); the page background matches theme-color in both themes; on a fresh visit, switched from the keyboard so
  the pointer never moves the photo, the ring round the button has already changed in the first changed frame (it
  fails when the origin is the middle); and on phones the card ends exactly 12px
  above the dock at the end of a page with only one scene drawn.
