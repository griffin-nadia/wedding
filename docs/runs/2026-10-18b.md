## 7. Our story transitions

- Before (filmed with `.brief/tools/story-film.mjs`): the stops sat on a native scroll-snap track, so a change was
  one rigid strip sliding sideways: print and words moved together, nothing turned or eased.
- Now the stops are stacked in one place (so the track is the tallest stop and nothing below it moves) and one
  spring moves between them (stiffness 170, damping 20: a hair past straight, then back, about 450ms):
  - the arriving print slides in from the right turned up to 8 degrees and settles straight;
  - the leaving print eases back (up to 8% smaller, a little left) and fades;
  - the chapter words cross-fade: out by halfway, in from halfway;
  - a drag moves it with the finger the whole way (mouse too); letting go past a quarter, or a flick, goes on,
    otherwise it springs back; a little give at either end. Arrows, dots and arrow keys use the same spring.
  - Written straight to the elements each frame on the shared frame loop, no re-render while moving; only the stops
    on screen draw.
- A swipe on the story no longer reaches the phone page turn (the track used to stop it by being a scroller; it now
  says so with `data-own-swipe`).
- Reduced motion: nothing moves, turns or follows the finger; a swipe or tap fades straight to the stop (200ms,
  opacity only).
- Checked at 390 (phone, mid-tap and mid-drag frames) and 1440, light and dark, motion on and off.
- Check: `e2e/story.spec.ts`. Mid-transition the arriving print is turned and its words part-faded, and at rest it's
  straight with only one stop drawn; a drag (touch on phones, mouse on laptops) shows the next stop under the finger,
  goes on when let go, and never turns the page; under reduced motion nothing turns or moves and the stop fades in.
- Merged as #107.
